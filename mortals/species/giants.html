Giants

Giants are creatures of Change. When the world was first made, the giants were there, to make mountains, valleys, rivers, and storms. When mortals began to walk the earth, the giants were there, guiding the to agriculture, magic, and warfare. And now, as mortal kingdoms vie for dominance and viziers seek to reshape the world, the giants are still here. They wander the earth, sometimes stopping to build a great runestone or shape the future of a kingdom. But wherever they are, they rarely stay. Giants are fickle creatures, and after a great victory or terrible conquest, they often simply leave, wandering the earth again without ever explaining why they stopped what they were doing.

Many have speculated as to the nature of the giants. The creatures themselves don't tend to explain their workings, leading many to believe that they are created divinely for some specific purpose. If they are, that purpose must be convoluted - giants fight each other often, and have been known to lend themselves to causes both evil and good. Still, the power they command is great enough that many worship them simply for a chance to gain their favor. The magic and craftsmanship of the giants is ancient and versatile - perhaps not as powerful as modern magic, but still capable of things no one else could dream of.

Giant physiology varies greatly, as they are a species made of pure elemental magic. Their bodies can be made of anything from flesh to stone to fungus, ranging from a mostly-human appearance to something that looks like a walking mountain. Though they lack the ability to reproduce, their species is in no danger of going extinct. When a giant dies, their corpse falls to the ground in a great, heaving crash - and a century or two later, they get back up again, seemingly unbothered by the experience. Nobody is sure what animates them, but magic placing a creature completely beyond death is extremely rare - they must be working with something beyond the comprehension of mortal powers.
